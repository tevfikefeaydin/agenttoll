# Five-user research pilot

Status: protocol ready; no participant sessions or outcomes have been recorded.

## Decision

Can a Base agent developer or researcher independently discover a candidate, understand the evidence and price, and return for a second useful observation? Recruit five consenting participants who already research Base tokens, including at least two API/MCP developers. The operator contacts participants; this document sends no messages.

Use the live release only after it is deployed and verified. Record the release SHA, date and whether the task used a fixture or real provider data. Never combine fixture completion with paid production usage.

## Session (20 minutes)

1. Ask what the participant currently does when evaluating a new Base token and what triggers a second check. Do not suggest a preferred answer.
2. Ask them to start at the homepage and find discovery. Before any wallet action, ask them to explain the price, network and what a discovery purchase includes.
3. They may use a pre-recorded operator demonstration without connecting a wallet. A real paid task is optional and requires the participant's own explicit approval of each exact quote. Do not request private keys or fund/move money on their behalf.
4. Ask them to choose a candidate, distinguish the token address from the pool address, and explain whether a listing establishes safety. Record where attribution or data is missing.
5. Ask them to inspect the recorded example, identify one source/date and one missing or conflicting check, save it, and reload the page. Examples remain labelled examples; do not pretend they are current paid results.
6. Ask them to explain the price of a new token inspection and how it differs from discovery. With their optional separate authorization, they can perform a real inspection and save it.
7. Ask them to locate the watchlist digest and download a reminder. Explain that the calendar app owns reminders after import and AgentToll does not check or pay in the background.
8. Arrange an optional follow-up after seven days. Ask whether they actually needed another inspection and what decision it informed. Do not count downloading a reminder as return usage.

## Review after the pilot

- Report unaided task completion as a count out of the sessions actually run, with assisted tasks separate. Five sessions are qualitative evidence, not a population conversion rate.
- Review external confirmed deliveries, gross USDC, signed failures, unresolved receipts and distinct returning wallets over the retained application-log window. Quote requests are not people or a conversion denominator.
- Compare repeated expressed needs with observed voluntary return use. Prioritize notification automation only if users have a concrete recurring need; first design durable spending authorization and unknown-payment reconciliation.
- Record provider invoice costs separately with their covered dates; gross USDC is not profit. Existing logs cannot reconstruct a provider invoice.
- Fix any observed misunderstanding of payment, safety or evidence before promoting the workflow more widely.

## Two-week operator schedule

Days 1–2: install and verify the private collector; retain daily aggregate reports and inspect coverage warnings. Days 3–7: run the five sessions and fix demonstrated blockers. Days 8–14: observe the follow-ups, compare retained usage with documented coverage and decide which next feature has evidence behind it. This schedule is a plan, not an enabled automation or a claim that participants exist.
